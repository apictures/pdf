Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FreePDF (“we”, “our” or “the website”) handles information when you use our browser-based PDF tools.

File handling: FreePDF is designed to process supported files in your browser. We do not operate a FreePDF file-upload server for these tools. Your browser may still contact third-party services to load libraries or other website features.

Files you use with FreePDF

For the tools currently provided, file processing is designed to happen locally in your browser. Files you select are not intentionally sent to a FreePDF backend. Keep in mind that your browser, extensions, device, or third-party scripts may operate independently of FreePDF.

Information and technical data

Our static website does not currently require you to create an account. The hosting provider and services embedded in the page may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, or request logs according to their own policies.

Third-party libraries and services

FreePDF loads software libraries and icons from third-party content delivery networks to provide website and conversion features. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the requested resource. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Advertising and cookies

At the time of this update, FreePDF does not serve a live advertising unit. The website contains a reserved space for a possible future banner placement, but that space does not itself load an ad. If advertising is introduced later, this policy should be updated to describe the provider, relevant data use, cookies or similar technologies, and any consent choices required by applicable law before advertising is enabled. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, though blocking some cookies may affect third-party features.

Children’s privacy

FreePDF is a general-purpose utility website and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use our Contact page.
