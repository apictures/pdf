About FreePDF

Your Files. Your Device. Your Privacy.

FreePDF is a collection of free browser-based tools designed to help people work with documents without installing a desktop application.

What can you do?

  • Convert images and supported office documents to PDF.
  • Extract text and work with supported PDF content.
  • Merge or split PDF files.
  • Use document scanning, ID-card layout, OCR, and PDF protection tools where supported.

Privacy by design

Our goal is to process supported files locally in your browser instead of uploading them to a FreePDF file-processing server. Files you select for these operations are handled on your device. The website may still load site assets or third-party libraries over the internet, so this is not a promise that the website makes no network requests at all. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

Built around your control

FreePDF does not require an account for its tools. Processing and results depend on the file, browser, available memory, and each tool’s capabilities.

Important limitations

Results depend on the file, browser, available memory, and the capabilities of each tool. Complex layouts, scanned documents, fonts, formulas, and password-protected files may not convert perfectly. Always review the output before relying on it.
