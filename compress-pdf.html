Compress PDF Online for Free

Your Files. Your Device. Your Privacy. FreePDF creates a smaller PDF by rendering each page and rebuilding the document with adjustable image quality. Processing happens in your browser; your PDF is not uploaded to a FreePDF file-processing server.

Compress PDF

Important: This method turns page contents into images. Text may no longer be selectable or searchable, and interactive links, forms, signatures, and accessibility information may not be retained. Keep your original PDF.

How to reduce PDF file size

  1. Open the Compress PDF tool above.
  2. Select a PDF from your device.
  3. Choose Balanced, Smallest file, or Higher quality.
  4. Download the result, compare file sizes, and review every page.

Choose a compression setting

  • Balanced: a middle ground between image detail and file size.
  • Smallest file: lower resolution and stronger JPEG compression; fine text may look softer.
  • Higher quality: keeps more visual detail, but the result may be larger.

Frequently asked questions

Will compression always make my PDF smaller?

No. Already-compressed or simple PDFs may not shrink. Compare the original and output sizes, and try another setting if needed.

Can I still select text after compression?

This version rebuilds pages as images, so text selection and search generally will not work in the compressed output.

Will links, forms, or digital signatures remain?

Interactive elements and digital signatures may not be preserved. Keep the original file, especially if it contains signed or fillable forms.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

Compression runs locally in your browser. FreePDF does not upload your PDF to a FreePDF file-processing server for this operation. Your browser still loads website assets and third-party libraries; see our Privacy Policy.
