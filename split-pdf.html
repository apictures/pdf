Split PDF Files Online for Free

Your Files. Your Device. Your Privacy. Need only a few pages from a larger PDF? Use FreePDF to select the pages you want to extract and create a new PDF in your browser. Page extraction runs locally on your device, without uploading your PDF to a FreePDF file-processing server.

Split a PDF

Before you start: Have your PDF ready. When the tool opens, choose the file, review the page previews, select the pages to keep, and choose Extract Selected Pages.

How to extract pages from a PDF

  1. Open the FreePDF Split PDF tool using the button above.
  2. Select one PDF file from your device.
  3. Use the page previews to select the pages you want in the new file.
  4. Choose Extract Selected Pages and download the resulting PDF.

Why use FreePDF to split a PDF?

  • Free to use: No paid conversion plan is required.
  • Choose specific pages: Select individual pages or a group of pages using the previews.
  • No account required: Open the tool directly from the website.
  • Local processing: Page extraction runs in your browser without uploading your PDF to a FreePDF file-processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract just one page from a PDF?

Yes. Select only the page you need, then run the extraction.

Can I select multiple pages?

Yes. Select any combination of available pages in the page preview before processing.

Do I need to install software?

No desktop installation is required. Use a modern browser and open FreePDF.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

Page extraction runs locally in your browser; your PDF is not uploaded to a FreePDF file-processing server. The website may still load site assets and third-party libraries over the internet. See our Privacy Policy for details.
