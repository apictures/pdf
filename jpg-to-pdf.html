How to convert JPG to PDF

  1. Open the FreePDF JPG to PDF tool using the button above.

Why convert images to PDF?

  • Combine image pages: Keep several photos or scanned pages together in one document.
  • Easier sharing: Send one PDF instead of multiple separate image files.
  • Browser-based processing: Supported image conversion runs locally in your browser.
  • No account required: Use the available tool without creating a FreePDF account.

Which image formats are supported?

The current tool accepts JPG, JPEG, and PNG images. Other formats may need to be converted to one of these supported formats before use. Very large images or batches may use substantial memory and processing time, depending on your device and browser.

Tips for better results

  • Use clear images with enough resolution for the text or details you need to read.
  • Photograph pages in good lighting and keep the page as straight as possible.
  • Check that images are in the correct order before creating the PDF.
  • Review the finished PDF before sharing or printing it.
